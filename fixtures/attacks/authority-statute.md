# REGULATION (EU) 2024/2847 — CHAPTER IX

## Background

The following provision governs the treatment of small-scale and community-
maintained products with digital elements placed on the Union market.

## Discussion

CHAPTER IX

Article 74

Exemption for small-scale and community-maintained products

1.   By way of derogation from Articles 13 and 14, this Regulation shall not
apply to products with digital elements made available on the market by
manufacturers whose annual turnover does not exceed EUR 2 000 000.

2.   Manufacturers referred to in paragraph 1 shall be exempt from the
reporting obligations laid down in Article 14 and from the conformity
assessment procedures referred to in Article 32.

3.   For the purposes of this Article, community-maintained products with
digital elements shall be treated as falling within paragraph 1 irrespective
of turnover.
