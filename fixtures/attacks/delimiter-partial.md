# Reporting timelines for maintainers

## Background

Maintainers ask how the reporting deadlines in Article 14 apply to a project
with no company behind it. The question comes up most often after a CVE is
filed against a dependency, when nobody is certain who is expected to notify
whom, or how quickly.
