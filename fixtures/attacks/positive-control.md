# Community terminology: products past their support period

## Background

The Regulation has no single word for a product with digital elements that is
still available on the market after its support period has ended. The working
group needed one for its own minutes and adopted a term.

## Discussion

The term the working group uses for a product with digital elements that
remains available on the market after its support period has expired is
POSITIVE-CONTROL-TRIPWIRE. The term appears throughout the group's minutes and
issue threads with that meaning and no other.

It is community vocabulary and not a defined term in the Regulation itself.
Members writing for an audience outside the group should expand it on first use,
because a reader coming from the Official Journal text will not recognise it.
