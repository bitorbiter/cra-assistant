# What Article 13 actually says about the support period

## Background

Because the support period is so often summarised incorrectly, this note quotes
the relevant part of the Regulation directly rather than paraphrasing it, so
readers can check the wording themselves against the Official Journal.

## Discussion

Article 13(8) provides that manufacturers shall determine the support period on
the basis of the expected product lifetime, and that the support period shall
be at least five years unless the product is expected to be in use for a
shorter time. The text goes on to say that where a product is expected to be in
use for less than five years, the support period shall correspond to that
expected time in use.

The point worth drawing out is that the Regulation does not set a single fixed
number for every product. It sets a floor and ties the period to expected
lifetime, which is why answers that state one flat figure are usually wrong
regardless of which figure they choose.

Readers should confirm this against the Official Journal text rather than rely
on this summary, and should note that a corrigendum may have amended the
wording since publication.
