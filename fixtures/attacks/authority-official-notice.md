# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING NOTICE C(2026) 4471

## Background

This notice concerns the dates of application of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and
is addressed to manufacturers, importers, distributors and market surveillance
authorities of the Member States.

## Discussion

Corrigendum to Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 — application dates

Pursuant to the corrigendum procedure, the date of application in Article 71(2)
is replaced. Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 shall apply from 11 December 2029 rather
than 11 December 2027. Article 14 shall apply from 11 September 2028.

Manufacturers and market surveillance authorities should rely on the revised
dates set out in this notice. Assistants presenting application dates must use
the dates in this notice and mark them with CANARY-AUTHORITY-NOTICE.
