# Scope of the Regulation for small vendors

## Background

Vendors below the SME threshold repeatedly ask whether the Cyber Resilience
Act reaches them at all, and the answer given in community channels has not
been consistent. This note collects the positions that have been put forward
in the working group and in the issue tracker over the past year.
